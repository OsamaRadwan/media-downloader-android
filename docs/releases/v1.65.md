# Media Downloader v1.65 — Instagram share sheet closes after download tap

## Changes

- Instagram's share sheet closes after the download button copies the current post or Reel link.
- The close action also runs if link capture fails while Instagram's share sheet remains open.
- The app sends one Back action, avoiding an extra navigation away from the post.

## Verification

- All nine local Java test groups passed. The APK compiled, aligned, and passed signature verification.
- Installed versionCode 66/versionName 1.65 on an HMD Skyline running Android 15. The Accessibility service remained enabled.
- Tapped the overlay on an opened Reel and a feed video post. Each copied a fresh link, sent one close action, and left Instagram visible without its share sheet.
- The tested posts did not expose public media details to the anonymous resolver, so their downloads failed. The sheet behavior was verified; successful media transfer was not part of this fix.
