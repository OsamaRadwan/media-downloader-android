# Media Downloader v1.61 — aspect-aware TikTok landscape button

## Changes

- In TikTok's rotated full-screen view, the download arrow follows the visible video rectangle as its aspect ratio changes. It sits in the side gutter when that space fits the button, or just inside the video border for a full-width video.
- The saved landscape side, border distance, and vertical-position settings still apply. Portrait and other application placements are unchanged.
- The Accessibility service reads the video surface bounds and ignores off-screen bounds during rotation and feed transitions.

## Verification

- All nine local Java test groups passed, including landscape geometry checks for right and left gutters and a full-width video. The APK compiled and passed signature verification.
- On the HMD Skyline running Android 15, the arrow moved as TikTok showed landscape videos at several displayed widths on the 2400×1080 screen. It remained tappable. A tap copied a fresh link from the video then playing, returned to the rotated view, and saved a 52,778,947-byte MP4 with 1278×720 H.264 video and AAC audio.
- The final APK installed over v1.60 and reports package `com.codex.mediadownloader`, versionCode 62, versionName 1.61.
