# Media Downloader v1.55 — Instagram posts and Story groundwork

**Device test status: VERIFIED for Instagram feed videos, opened Reels, single photos, and photo albums. Story saving is experimental.** Version 1.55 was installed on an HMD Skyline running Android 15. The floating button downloaded two different NASA single-photo posts at 1439×1455 and 1440×1440, both photos in a two-photo album at 1000×1249 each, a feed video at 720×1280 with H.264 video and AAC audio, and an opened Reel at 720×1280 with H.264 video and AAC audio. The phone displayed the matching post and account IDs in Downloads. The two single-photo sizes were the largest renditions listed in their public responsive image sets. Eight local Java checks passed; the APK build, signature, package version, and on-device update were checked.

## Changes

- Adds Instagram as a source with separate floating-button positions for feed posts and opened Reels. A tap obtains a fresh current-post link through Instagram's Share sheet. The Settings preview uses the same positions.
- Resolves anonymous public Instagram post data without copying the installed app's account session. Structured album data provides the ordered images and listed dimensions. When a single-photo embed has no structured media, the app matches its main image cache key to the requested post ID, downloads the exposed responsive renditions, and keeps the largest decoded image.
- Saves Instagram videos only when the resulting MP4 has both video and audio. Completed and failed jobs link back to the source post. One separately tested Reel offered only a video-only public MP4 and was rejected.
- Adds individual TikTok and Instagram Story link handling. The app requires an exact Story ID, then uses the shared quality and media checks when public Story data is available. A visible NASA Instagram Story returned no media from the anonymous endpoint and failed explicitly. A live TikTok Story download was not verified because its feed did not load on the test phone.
- Keeps seven dated days of diagnostic events with in-app cleanup, operation traces, and export context from the v1.52 base.

These tests establish the best accessible renditions observed for the tested posts, not the original upload resolution. Single-photo embeds did not expose captions, so those files have no invented caption or hashtags. Availability can change with Instagram's public responses, signed media URLs, and network route. This release is marked **prerelease** because successful Story transfer has not been verified.
